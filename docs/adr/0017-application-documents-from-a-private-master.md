# ADR 0017: application documents from a private master CV

**Status:** accepted (2026-11-01, M26, parts 1 and 2). Amends ADR 0016 §12 ("out of scope: CV generation") and ADR 0011's rejection of R2, and ARCHITECTURE §10 ("we do not build CV generation"): career-ops stays a manual companion for deep evaluation and interview prep; the documents themselves are generated here, from a source only this repo's owner controls.

## Context

Every offer I keep ("Garder" in the Tri, status « À candidater ») needs a CV tailored to it and a cover letter. Done by hand, that is the slowest step of the pipeline and the one that drifts: a CV rewritten for each offer ends up claiming things the last version did not. The constraints are the ones of CLAUDE.md: nothing personal leaves this machine except toward Cloudflare's private storage; offer text is untrusted; the core (jobs, funding, dashboard) works without this; no invention.

## Decision

1. **One master, private, in YAML.** `private/cv_master.yaml` holds the experiences, the bullets with stable IDs (`exp1.b3`), the tools each bullet cites, the canonical skills (`skill_vocabulary.yaml`, `aliases.yaml` keys, the profile's skills), the skill groups for display, the education, the certifications, the languages, the projects, and three facts for the Profil paragraph (`target`, `availability`, `mobility`). `private/contact.yaml` holds the identity (names, title, email, phone, LinkedIn, GitHub, location) and nothing else. `private/` is gitignored; the files are never a fixture, never in a log.
   - **Validator** (`job-match cv validate`, `src/job_match/documents/validate.py`): every skill resolves to a canonical name; no phrase of `settings.yaml documents.forbidden_phrases` (« sans régression », « zéro régression », « 0 régression », « 63 dimensions »: true once, but a claim I no longer want to carry); no email, phone, URL nor the candidate's name inside the master (contact.yaml only); ISO `YYYY-MM` dates, an end after its start, unique IDs. Counts and error lines only on the console.
   - **Push** (`job-match cv push`, by hand, never from a workflow): both files become sorted JSON objects `master/cv_master.json` and `master/contact.json` in the private R2 bucket `job-match-docs`, through `npx wrangler r2 object put` (the tool already used for D1 and the deploy; the local `wrangler login` suffices, no S3 key pair anywhere). Alternatives: SigV4 over httpx or boto3, each a new secret pair for one file.
2. **Generation in the Worker's cron, every ten minutes** (`worker/src/documents.js`, trigger `*/10 * * * *` next to the rerank's two; `scheduled()` branches on `event.cron`). A "Garder" (`PUT /api/applications/:key` with `to_apply`) inserts one `documents` row `pending` when the offer has none; « Regénérer » (`POST /api/documents/:key/regenerate`) adds the next version (409 while one is pending or running). Each pass takes five pending rows, oldest first, and for each re-checks that the application still stands (`to_apply`, `applied` or `interview`; otherwise `failed: decision_gone`) and that the offer is still in `data.json` (`jobs[]` or `tracked[]`; otherwise `offer_gone`). A pass with nothing pending leaves no row and no log line.
   - **Model:** `claude-sonnet-5-5` (platform.claude.com, checked 2026-11-01: $2 per million input tokens, $10 per million output, cache reads 5 % of the input price; structured outputs through `output_config.format` of type `json_schema`, no beta header, `additionalProperties: false` required). Plain `fetch`, `anthropic-version: 2026-10-21`, `effort: medium`, thinking left to the model. No Workers AI fallback: a cover letter in Llama's non-guaranteed JSON is not worth it; the rows stay pending.
   - **What the model receives:** one cached system block (the instructions and the master JSON with sorted keys) and the offer between `<offre>` tags with the expected language. The master carries no contact field by construction, and `contact.json` is never read by the generation path (a test keeps `contact.json` out of `runDocuments`). A test sends a contact sheet full of trap values through the whole path and checks the request body and the log.
   - **Output, strict JSON (prompt version 2, after the review of the first two real documents):** `language` (fr by default, en when English function words dominate the offer), `profile` (2 to 3 sentences: who, what I look for, availability), `selected_bullets` (`{id, text}`, the text a reformulation of that bullet; the order only matters inside one experience), `skills` (12 to 15, the offer's first, then the DevOps core; no development language or framework unless the offer names it), `letter.paragraphs` (3 to 5), each a list of sentences `{text, sources}` where `sources` are the IDs of the master bullets the sentence draws its facts from. No salutation, closing or signature: the template adds them from `contact.json`.
   - **Validator, before anything is stored** (`validateDocument`): every bullet ID exists once; a reformulation holds no number absent from its source bullet; the profile holds no number absent from the master; a capitalised word inside a sentence that is neither in the master nor in the offer is an unknown name (an employer, a place, a product); `skills` are among the master's skills, tools and group items, 15 at most, no `DEV_LANGUAGES` entry absent from the offer; forbidden phrases; lengths and counts; the language. **Per letter sentence:** every number comes from its cited bullets (or the headline's availability), every tool term of the master named in the sentence comes from its cited bullets (the headline's and the skills list's terms excepted), and an employer or a context word of an experience title (« alternance », « stage », « projet de fin d'études ») names an experience the sentence cites, so « en alternance, j'ai mis en place Prometheus » fails when Prometheus belongs to the internship. A salutation at the start or a closing anywhere is `letter_formula`. On errors, one retry with the error list appended as a user turn after the model's answer; then `failed` with the first error's code (`invented_number`, `invented_skill`, `unknown_name`, `unknown_bullet`, `unsourced_tool`, `wrong_context`, `letter_formula`, `too_many_skills`, `dev_language`, `forbidden_phrase`, `too_long`, `bad_shape`, `wrong_language`, `invalid_json`, `refused`, `api_error`).
   - **Storage:** `docs/<job_key>/v<version>/content.json` in R2: the validated JSON, the offer's title and company, and `cv` (the experiences from the most recent to the oldest with their selected bullets in the model's order, the projects likewise), ready for the renderer; the row becomes `ready` with the tokens and the cost. The rendered PDF and DOCX keys (part 3) are columns already.
3. **Budget.** `AI_DOCUMENTS_MONTHLY_USD` (wrangler var, 60 by default; `settings.yaml documents.monthly_budget_usd` repeats it, a test keeps them equal) caps the month's estimated spend: `ai_usage` now carries the model (D1 migration 0005 rebuilds it with the primary key `(day, provider, model)`), so the Sonnet tokens are priced at Sonnet rates and the Haiku ones at Haiku rates (`ai/report.py PRICES_PER_M`). The estimate counts cache reads at the full input price: an upper bound. Over budget, the pass stops and the rows stay pending. A 402 or a 429 leaves the row pending, records a `credit` run and pauses the documents cron for six hours: the rerank keeps whatever credit is left. Any other 4xx stops the pass with its status, like the rerank. Only billed answers (HTTP 200) count.
4. **Observability.** One log line per pass that did something (`documents ok {counters}`, counters only), one `document_runs` row, and `GET /api/applications` returns the latest version per offer (status, error code, never a key) for the record panel's « Documents » block and the « Regénérer » button. The mirror export carries `model` in `ai_usage` (SQLite migration 0011).
5. **Smoke test** (`scripts/docs-smoke.mjs`): migrations applied to the local D1, `job-match cv push --local` to the local R2, pending rows for two offers of `site/data.json`, one cron pass through `wrangler dev --test-scheduled`, the `content.json` files copied to `private/out/`, the tokens and the dollars printed per document.
6. **Prompt version 3 (2026-11-01, after the review of the v2 documents).**
   - **Tense from the dates.** An experience whose `end` is on or before the current month is over: the user block lists them, the model tells them in the past tense, and the validator refuses the first-person present (`wrong_tense`: a curated verb list, « actuellement », « en cours », "currently"…) in a letter sentence that cites only finished experiences or names one, and in the Profil when no experience is ongoing. A heuristic, documented as such.
   - **Availability from the master and the day.** `available_from` (`YYYY-MM-DD`, Python-validated) replaces the free `availability` text: the Worker computes « Disponible immédiatement » / « Disponible à partir de <mois année> » (English likewise) from today's date, gives it in the user block, and the validator requires it verbatim in the Profil with no other availability claim (`availability_missing`).
   - **Skills as catalog ids.** The master's `skills` is a catalog `[{id, label}]`; an id is canonical (vocabulary, alias key, profile skill) or a tool cited by a bullet. The model answers with ids only, 12 to 15; `content.json` stores `{id, label}` and the template prints the label. The headline's tools are the only ones a sentence may name without a source: a catalog id is a skill to list, not a fact to tell.
   - **No second person in the Profil** (`profile_address`).
   - **First-attempt codes.** `documents.first_errors` (migration 0006) keeps the validator codes of attempt 1; `docs-smoke` prints them per document and the version history, and judges only the latest version per offer.
6b. **Prompt version 4 (2026-11-01, after the v3 documents).** The availability is a full sentence ending with a period in the Profil, and appears once in the whole letter, integrated in a sentence of the last paragraph (`availability_misplaced`); the last paragraph is the conclusion alone, no sentence with sources (`conclusion_facts`); a Profil that links experiences with a time connector (« puis », « ensuite »…) names them in the order of their dates (`profile_chronology`); the skills the offer asks for that the chosen bullets prove come first (`skills_order`). **Targeted retry:** when every error sits in a profile, a bullet, a letter sentence or the skills list, only those items go back to the model with their codes (`buildTargetedRetry`, `RETRY_SCHEMA`), the answer replaces them in the first document (`mergeRetry`) and the merged document is validated again; a document-level error (a paragraph count, the language) still gets the full retry. `scripts/docs-smoke.mjs --render-only` renders the latest ready version of each offer locally (render.js and the Inter font, no `wrangler dev`), stores the files in the local R2 and copies the JSON, the PDFs and the DOCX of every version into `private/out/`; the normal run copies the same files after the cron.
6c. **A dense one-page CV (2026-11-01, after the first rendered v3; reference: the original CV).** The model orders and reformulates every bullet that fits the offer and drops only the off-topic ones (`MAX_BULLETS` 18; an experience with four bullets or more keeps at least four, `too_few_bullets`). The skills come as the master's display groups, `skill_groups: [{group, items}]` copied verbatim, seven at most, in the model's order for the offer (`invented_skill`, `too_many_skills`, `skills_order`: the first group holds something the offer asks for; `dev_language` on an item naming only development languages the offer does not); the title line is `contact.title + " — " + headline_techs` (5–6 technologies the offer asks for and the master holds, `headline_techs`). Bold is the template's: `content.highlights` (the master's tool terms the offer names) and the key numbers (15+, 30 merge requests, 80 %), as mixed runs in the PDF and `<w:b/>` runs in the DOCX; the model is told not to format. Certifications are not rendered (kept in the master); Engagement (`extras`) is a section. The renderer fills one page: every variant (projects all → none, Engagement on/off) at 10.5, 10 and 9.5 pt, the first with 90 % or more of the page wins, else the best one-page fill, else the text is cut at page one; `renderCvPdfInfo` reports pages, fill, size and the levers used, `docs-smoke` prints them, and a test checks a CV of the original's volume lands on one page at 90 % or more.
7. **Part 3, ATS rendering in the Worker** (`worker/src/render.js`, pure; `renderReady` in `documents.js`, called by the documents cron after the generation). **Superseded by ADR 0019 (2026-11-04):** the Worker exceeded the Free plan's 10 ms of CPU (error 1102); the same `render.js` now runs in the browser, the Worker only serves `content.json`. The layout, names and tests below still describe the template.
   - **Libraries, the Worker's first npm dependencies** (`worker/package.json`, `npm ci --prefix worker` in `ci.yml` and before the deploy in `daily.yml`): `pdf-lib` + `@pdf-lib/fontkit` to embed a real font (Inter 4.1 static Regular and Bold, OFL, `worker/fonts/`, bundled as bytes through a wrangler `Data` rule) so the accents are text; `fflate` to zip a hand-written OOXML DOCX. Alternatives: a hand-written PDF writer (font embedding alone is a project), the `docx` package (Node streams, no Workers build). The font module is imported lazily: Node cannot import a `.ttf`, so the tests pass the bytes read from disk and `renderReady` skips rendering without them.
   - **Layout:** A4, 18 mm margins, one column, Inter 10 pt, bold capitalised headings in this order: Profil, Expérience (reverse chronological from `content.cv`), Compétences (labels), Formation, Projets, Langues, Certifications (English headings for `en`). Real text, no image, icon, table or column. One page aimed at, two at most: over that the projects go, then the certifications, then the type shrinks to 9 pt, and as a last resort the text is cut at the end of page two. The letter carries the identity block, the date, the company, the subject, « Madame, Monsieur, », the model's paragraphs, the closing formula and the signature, all from `contact.json` and the template.
   - **Storage and download:** `docs/<key>/v<n>/{cv.pdf,cv.docx,letter.pdf,letter.docx}` in R2 with the file name as custom metadata (`CV_<Prénom>_<Nom>_<Entreprise>.pdf`, `Lettre_…`, ASCII-folded), served by `GET /api/documents/:key/v<n>/<file>` behind Access and same-origin, as an attachment, `no-store`. A render failure keeps the content and sets `render_error`; the next pass retries. `GET /api/applications` says `files: true` once they exist.
   - **Tests:** the PDF text is extracted with `pdfjs-dist` (headings in order, keywords, accents, the contact details present), the DOCX unzipped (well-formed, headings in order, escaped), the two-page cap, the route headers and names.
8. **Part 4, the applications as a table.** A Kanban / Tableau toggle in the Candidatures header (`aria-pressed` chips, remembered in `localStorage`, `#candidatures?view=table` in the hash): the table has the columns Offre (opens the record), Entreprise, Score IA, Statut (the same menu as the cards), CV and Lettre (PDF and DOCX downloads, or the documents badge until the files exist), Lien, Relance (due ones first). The badge also sits on every Kanban card and in the record panel next to « Regénérer ». The logic is in `tracking.js` (`docBadge`, `documentLinks`) and tested in Node; the DOM is checked in the browser.
   - **Ready means downloadable (2026-11-02, prod bug: « Prêt (v1) » rows with no links).** `GET /api/documents/:key/v<n>/<file>` renders the four files from `content.json` on the first request when the row has no file keys yet (`renderRow`, the same function the cron batch uses; `loadFonts`), stores them and serves the one asked for; a render failure answers 503 and leaves `render_error` on the row. The cron batch orders the never-rendered rows before the ones that failed once, so a stuck row no longer blocks the five slots. The client links whenever the version is `ready`; the lone « Prêt » badge is gone from the table.
   - **The cron queues « À candidater » by itself (2026-11-02).** At the start of every pass `runDocuments` calls `requestMissingDocuments` with `statuses: ["to_apply"]` and `limit: BATCH_SIZE`: one pending v1 per « À candidater » application without any documents row, within the budget, and the same pass takes them. The buttons stay manual for the other statuses and for a failed row. `counters.queued` in `document_runs`.
   - **« Générer » runs now (2026-11-02).** `POST /api/documents/:key/regenerate` still answers 201 with the pending row, then hands `runDocumentsScheduled(env, {keys: [key]})` to `ctx.waitUntil`: the pass for that offer only (no auto-queue, same budget and credit rules, its own `document_runs` row), then the render. Without a ctx (the Node tests) the row waits for the cron. The table cell reads « En cours… » and `watchDocuments` refetches `/api/applications` every 10 s, 12 times at most, while a watched offer is pending or running, rebuilding only `track.documents`; a Kanban chip bug (pressed from the markup while the remembered view was the table) is fixed by `setAppsView(appsView, true)` at boot.
   - **Generation on demand from the table (2026-11-02).** « Générer » on a row without any documents row and « Regénérer » next to « Échec » (the error code in the tooltip) call the existing `POST /api/documents/:key/regenerate`; both show only for a kept status (`KEEP_STATUSES`) on a listed offer, since the pass fails otherwise (`decision_gone`, `offer_gone`). « Générer les manquants (N) » above the table asks for one pending v1 per kept application without a row through `POST /api/documents/missing`, after a held toast that states the count and the estimated cost (« 7 documents · ≈ 0,42 $ (reste 58,10 $ ce mois) »): the Worker caps the request by the remaining budget at the estimate per document: the mean `cost_micro_usd` of the last 20 billed documents (the same tokens `ai_usage` sums by day; pending rows excluded), or `DEFAULT_ESTIMATE_PER_DOCUMENT_USD` (0.08, `settings.yaml documents.default_estimate_per_document_usd`) while there is no history and answers `{requested, skipped_budget, remaining_usd, documents}`; nothing is launched, the cron or « Lancer maintenant » takes the rows. A failed row is not part of « manquants »: it usually needs a look at its code first. `GET /api/applications` carries `documents_budget` (`monthly_usd`, `spent_usd`, `remaining_usd`, `estimate_usd`), read-only, so the dashboard never computes a price. Alternatives: a modal (no dialog in the system, the held toast is the pattern), a separate budget endpoint (one more call before every render).
   - **Downloads from the table (2026-11-02).** The CV and Lettre cells show « ⬇ PDF » and « ⬇ DOCX » links to the Worker route; the browser follows `Content-Disposition: attachment` and saves `CV_<Prénom>_<Nom>_<Entreprise>.pdf` in place, no new tab. The links carry **no `download` attribute**: with one, Chromium names the file after the URL (`cv.pdf`) instead of the header. Browser test: `page.expect_download()`, the suggested name and the `%PDF-` magic.
   - **Widths (2026-11-02).** `table.data.apps` is `fixed` with a `<colgroup>` (Entreprise 16 %, Score IA 72 px, Statut 196 px, CV and Lettre 126 px each, Lien 76 px, Relance 150 px; Offre takes the rest and ellipsizes with the full title in its tooltip); Statut, CV, Lettre and Relance never clip. A missing company reads « Non communiquée » in tertiary ink. At 390 the rows stack with a label per cell (`data-label`). Browser test at 1440 and 390: no element under those cells with `scrollWidth > clientWidth`, no horizontal page scroll.

## Amendment 2026-11-04: prompt v5 (headline_techs, profile_chronology)

Prod 2026-11-02/03: 14 of 29 failed rows carried `headline_techs` and 7 `profile_chronology` (`first_errors` on every one of the 14). D1 keeps no model text, so the rules were replayed on the offers and the master:

- **`headline_techs` asked for 5 to 6 technologies present in the master and the offer.** Of the 7 offers still in the local base, 6 named fewer than 5 master skills (1, 2, 3, 4, 4, 4; one named 6). The model was forced to add one absent from the offer. Now the Worker computes `headlineCandidates(master, offer)` (the master's skill labels the offer names, deduplicated), tells the model the list in the user message, and requires `min(5, candidates) to 6` techs, each in the master, in the offer, and once. Nothing becomes inventable: the list comes from the master.
- **`profile_chronology` read the text order as the dates' order** for every connector (`puis, ensuite, avant, après…`). « Ingénieur DevOps en alternance chez Atos, après un projet de fin d'études chez Orange » is true (the most recent first) and was refused; and `stagiaire`, a context word of two experiences in the real master, « named » both at one position, so any connector tripped the rule. Now only `puis / ensuite / then / later` are checked, and a context word two experiences share orders nothing (`orderTerms`). Chronology is an order rule, not an invention gate: numbers, names, skills, bullets and sources keep their own rules.
- Prompt: « Le profil cite l'expérience la plus récente d'abord ; « puis » et « ensuite » n'enchaînent que dans l'ordre réel des dates ; « après » et « avant » gardent leur sens » and the headline rule above. `DOCUMENTS_PROMPT_VERSION = "5"`, `settings.yaml documents.prompt_version: "5"`. One Node test per real case (three-skill offer, one-skill offer, no-skill offer, « après » recent-first, two « Stagiaire » experiences). The 14 rows are put back to `pending` by hand once the v5 Worker is deployed.

## Cost

Per document: about 4 000 tokens of cached system block (the master), about 2 000 tokens of offer, about 1 500 tokens of output; one retry doubles it. At list price: about $0.03 without retry, $0.06 with one; cache reads bring the real figure down. Ten kept offers a week: under $2 a month. The 60 $ budget is a ceiling, not a target.

## Consequences

- `private/` is a second private source next to `config/profile.yaml`; it never reaches CI. `cv push` is the only path out, by hand.
- `ai_usage` rows written before migration 0005 are attributed to the Haiku model (the rerank was the only writer).
- ADR 0016 §12 is amended: CV and cover-letter generation are in scope from this ADR; emails written by the AI stay out.
